# Recovery Inventory and Twelva: public-site review

Reviewed November 19, 2026. This is a review of public positioning and advertised features, not a test of Twelva's private app or evidence of conversion rates.

## Coverage and sources

Reviewed the homepage and feature/gallery sections; download flow; pricing, FAQ, founder story, editorial standards, resources and support; Sponsor Companion; all six advertised recovery-path pages; privacy, terms and deletion pages; three comparison pages and three app-selection guides; press and creator resources; the library catalogue and all seven category hubs; representative guides, glossary pages and tools; and public app-store listings. ## What matters for this product

| Area | Twelva advertises | Recovery Inventory today | Practical implication |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Focus | A broader companion across several recovery paths | Focused Step 4 workbooks and Step 10 pattern review with a human sponsor | Keep the focus that existing feedback values; avoid adding every recovery path just to match a list. |
| First use | Ongoing free journaling/reflection and a small daily AI allowance | Real free accounts, now 30 days and up to 30 saved daily Step 10 entries; optional paid upgrade | Clearly explain the free month and show a useful first-entry outcome. Free AI would be a separate product/cost decision. |
| Pricing | Terms list $4.99/month; annual store prices differ ($36.99 Google Play, $35.90 Apple) | $4.99/month or $25/year, USD | The annual offer is already lower. Improve demonstration and onboarding before cutting prices. Store checkout remains authoritative for Twelva's prices. |
| Presentation | Phone imagery, a gallery and an approachable daily-use story | Calm branding, an interactive demo, illustrative pattern examples and approved user feedback | Add a short, real screen recording and three actual demo screenshots showing entry → patterns → sponsor report. Label all sample data. |
| Daily practice | A simple daily return experience | Detailed 39-question daily inventory and existing weekly writing insights | Offer an optional brief check-in alongside the complete inventory and make existing weekly insights easier to find. |
| Sponsor use | A sponsor companion positioning | Chosen principles/notes, preview, PDF/image/text and no recipient account required | Give sponsors a dedicated explanation page with a clearly labelled sample report. |
| Privacy | Advertised on-device storage and optional AI | Encrypted server account storage, selective sharing and user-chosen AI submissions | Explain this product's actual model. Add straightforward self-service export/deletion controls rather than borrowing another product's storage claims. |
| Discovery | A substantial educational library and comparison material | Focused guides, comparison pages and a Step 10 app-intent page, now expanded into ten languages | Grow practical, specific guides around real Step 10 examples, Step 4 worksheets, recurring patterns and sponsor conversations. |

## Recommended order after this release

1. Add a 30–45-second real demo tour and three labelled screenshots. This makes the journal-to-pattern-to-sponsor workflow concrete before signup.
2. Guide the first entry, then surface the weekly insights that already exist. On a phone, keep the journal ahead of secondary account/settings controls. Preserve user-written drafts.
3. Add a sponsor resource page and sample one-page report, plus clear account export/delete controls.
4. Consider optional mood/HALT context, gentle opt-in reminders and a home-screen shortcut after observing demand. These should support reflection without implying prediction or clinical assessment.

These priorities are product judgments from the public review and the site's existing features and feedback. They are not measured promises of more visitors, subscriptions or search rankings. No external ratings, adoption totals or testimonials should be invented. Existing analytics, sponsor reports, weekly writing views, founder story and six approved quotes already provide useful differentiation.
